# Attribution (designed, not run)

**Status:** designed

- **Hypothesis:** follow-ups sent through the workflow raise 14-day reply + meeting rate compared with
  matched accounts that did not use it.
- **Baseline:** last-touch attribution, which credits every reply to the most recent email.
- **Method:** holdout accounts (randomly withheld from the workflow), or a matched comparison on
  tier, stage, owner, renewal window and initial health.
- **Metric:** 14-day reply rate and meeting-booked rate, holdout vs treated.
- **Minimum sample:** 300 accounts per group, enough to detect a 7-point lift from a 25% base rate.
- **Why not run:** needs real outcomes over weeks. The Outcome Tracker records them manually until then.
