# Reference retrieval (designed, not run)

**Status:** designed

- **Hypothesis:** retrieving past drafts ranked by similarity × validated outcome × evidence quality
  lowers the edit rate per draft compared with similarity-only retrieval.
- **Metric:** edit rate per draft (`approved_edited` / approved), per arm.
- **Design:** alternate arms per workflow; same accounts; reps blind to arm.
- **Minimum sample:** 200 decided drafts per arm, enough to detect a 10-point drop from a 40% edit rate at 80% power.
- **Why not run:** needs live reps producing real decisions and outcomes.
